About Renting

01.

How much does it cost to rent the space?

The current rental fee is $350 for up to 5 hours. Please contact us for the most up-to-date pricing, as the rental rate may increase to $450.

02.

What is included with the rental fee?

The rental includes access to several amenities to help make your event easier and more convenient, including:

  • Tables and chairs
  • Kitchenette
  • Refrigerator
  • Oven for warming food
  • Cocktail area
  • Bathroom access
  • Key access during your setup and event
  • Access to an event planner
  • Projector and speakers

03.

What is the maximum occupancy?

The space can accommodate up to 200 guests.

About the space

01.

Is catering allowed?

Yes! You are welcome to bring in your own food and catering. Decorations may also be brought in and set up for your event.

Alcohol is permitted; however, a liquor license is required to serve hard liquor. Please contact us for additional alcohol-related requirements.

02.

Do you set up the space for us?

Set-up is not typically included with the rental. Renters are generally responsible for setting up the space for their event. Please contact us to discuss your specific needs.

03.

What kind of parking is available?

Free parking is available on-site. If additional parking is needed, guests may also utilize the community park/community center parking area across the way, subject to availability.

Have questions or want to learn more?

Contact us to check availability, confirm current pricing, and discuss your event needs.
