About Us

Mission & Vision Statement

The Broadway Commons Event Center’s mission is to cultivate a beautiful, joyful space for families and organizations to gather, celebrate milestones, and create lasting memories rooted in a foundation of grace, while also providing a safe space for those in need throughout the community.

Reservations

Our spacious facility can comfortably seat up to 200 people, with 150 people seated at tables, and can be configured to meet your specific needs. And don’t forget to check out our inviting front porch! You’ll love the historic charm of our building, which was once a skating rink — the original floor is still visible throughout. We would be thrilled to provide you with more information, make a reservation, or schedule a tour of our space.
